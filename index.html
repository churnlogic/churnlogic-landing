What ChurnLogic does

A proactive companion that watches your deadlines, estimates real dollar values, and surfaces the decisions that matter — before you miss them.

Dollar values on everything

Every recommendation shows estimated dollar value and net first-year return after the annual fee, so you can compare cards in terms that actually matter.

Proactive action feed

Your home screen shows what needs attention now: MSR deadlines approaching, annual fee decisions due, velocity cooldowns ending, and 5/24 slots opening up.

Annual fee decisions

30–60 days before an annual fee hits, ChurnLogic shows your options: keep the card, downgrade to a no-fee version, or cancel — with specific product-change targets.

5/24 and drop dates

Tracks your Chase 5/24 status and shows exactly when each card drops off, so you know when to apply without guessing.

Issuer cooldowns

Per-issuer velocity timers for Chase, Amex, Citi, Barclays, and US Bank. Know exactly when you're eligible again instead of counting months manually.

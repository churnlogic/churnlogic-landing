Delete your ChurnLogic account

You can permanently delete your ChurnLogic account and all associated data at any time, directly from the app.

Delete from the app (instant)

  1. Open ChurnLogic and sign in.
  2. Tap the Settings gear (top right of the Home screen).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately and permanently.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes all of your data, including:

  • Your profile and login credentials
  • Every tracked card and application
  • Spend entries and bonus-tracking progress
  • Linked accounts and any imported credit-report data
  • Usage/analytics events tied to your account

There is no retention period — this data is removed at the time of deletion and is not recoverable. ChurnLogic never stores your Social Security number or full account numbers, so there is none to delete.

Can't access the app?

If you can no longer sign in, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete your account and data within 30 days:

support@churnlogic.app

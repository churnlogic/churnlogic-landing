For affiliate and issuer partners

ChurnLogic drives high-intent, compliant traffic from users actively planning well-timed credit card applications.

Quality over volume

ChurnLogic users are planning applications based on eligibility rules, cooling periods, and personal goals. They apply when they're ready, not on impulse. This means higher approval rates and lower complaint rates for issuer partners.

Compliance-first design

Every recommendation includes clear affiliate disclosures. ChurnLogic does not incentivize applications through coupons, bonuses, or manufactured spending advice. Recommendations are based on user eligibility, issuer rules, and stated goals.

What we do

  • Eligibility-aware recommendations
  • Clear affiliate disclosures on every card
  • Timing based on issuer velocity rules
  • Dollar-value estimates to set realistic expectations

What we don't do

  • No couponing or sign-up incentives
  • No manufactured spending advice
  • No pressure to apply before eligible
  • No misleading bonus representations
